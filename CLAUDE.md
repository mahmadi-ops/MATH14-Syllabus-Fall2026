# Claude workflow — MATH 14 Syllabus (Fall 2026)

PreTeXt article, published automatically on every push to `main`:

- Website: https://mahmadi-ops.github.io/MATH14-Syllabus-Fall2026/
- PDF: https://mahmadi-ops.github.io/MATH14-Syllabus-Fall2026/math14-syllabus-fall2026.pdf

Do **not** reorganize the sections, rename files, or restructure the article
unless the instructor asks.

Course materials are posted from the two companion repos, driven by the
instructor's MATH 14 Posting Desk: completed lecture notes from
`mahmadi-ops/MATH-14` (whose `CLAUDE.md` is the desk rulebook), and the
skeletal notes, assignments, review sets, and solutions from
`mahmadi-ops/MATH14-Skeletal`. Page names on both sites come from each
section's `xml:id` (e.g. `sec-parametrization.html`,
`sec-exercises-hw1-online.html`, `sec-exercises-review1-practice.html`).
This syllabus carries no posted-materials log; posting actions never touch
this repo. The section that points students at the posted materials is
"Textbook and Lecture Notes" in `source/materials.ptx`.

This repo was copied from `mahmadi-ops/MATH13-Syllabus-Fall2026`; the two
courses share the same exam dates, assignment due days, and final exam
period, and the same office hours.

Validate any edit with `xmllint --noout source/<file>.ptx` before
committing; publishing happens when the change reaches `main`.
